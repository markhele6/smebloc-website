Core Modules We Build

1. ePOD (Electronic Proof of Delivery)

Replace paper receipts with digital proof. Know exactly when and where a delivery was made.

  • Digital Signature & Photo: Drivers capture customer signatures and upload photos of the delivered goods.
  • Instant Updates: The main office dashboard updates in real-time.
  • Business Impact: Eliminates lost paper receipts and resolves customer disputes instantly.

2. Fleet & Driver Management

Track vehicle health, driver behavior, and expenses.

  • Vehicle Tracking: Live GPS integration to see where every truck is.
  • Fuel & Maintenance Logs: Track fuel expenses and set reminders for vehicle servicing.
  • Driver Settlements: Automatically calculate driver allowances and trip commissions.
  • Business Impact: Reduces fuel theft and prevents costly vehicle breakdowns.
