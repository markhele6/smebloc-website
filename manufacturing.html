Core Modules We Build

1. Real-Time Inventory & Stock Management

Know exactly what is in your warehouse at any given second. No more manual counting.

  • Raw Material Tracking: Track stock inward, outward, and current levels.
  • Low Stock Alerts: Automated WhatsApp/Email alerts when material is running out.
  • Barcode/QR Integration: Scan items for instant entry and dispatch.
  • Business Impact: Prevents production halts due to material shortage and stops over-purchasing.

2. Production Planning & QC Rejection Tracking

Track your production line efficiency and monitor quality control.

  • Batch Tracking: Assign a batch number to every production run.
  • QC Rejection Logs: When a product fails QC, log the rejection reason (e.g., machine fault, material defect).
  • Wastage Analysis: Calculate exactly how much material is wasted per batch.
  • Business Impact: Identifies bottlenecks in the factory and reduces wastage costs by up to 15%.

3. Dispatch & Billing System

Manage outbound shipments and generate invoices in one click.

  • Dispatch Scheduling: Assign vehicles, generate e-way bills, and print packing lists.
  • Automated Invoicing: Generate GST-compliant invoices directly from the dispatch data.
  • Ledger Management: Track pending payments from distributors and clients.
  • Business Impact: Speeds up dispatch time and eliminates billing errors.
